# DiscussionBridge

**Publish on your site. Discuss on your forum.**

Add Discourse discussions to your website, or publish between your site and
your forum. Start with the platform you use.

| Project | Use it to | V4 Alpha source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DiscussionBridge for Discourse](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/discourse-discussion-bridge) | Manage what can publish between your forum and your sites. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.receiver.2`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/discourse-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.receiver.2) |
| [DiscussionBridge for Ghost](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/ghost-discussion-bridge) | Add Discourse discussions to Ghost articles and publish in both directions. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.ghost-adapter.1`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/ghost-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.ghost-adapter.1) |
| [DiscussionBridge Ghost Connector](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/discourse-discussion-bridge-ghost) | Set up Ghost publishing and linked discussions from Discourse. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.ghost.3`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/discourse-discussion-bridge-ghost/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.ghost.3) |
| [DiscussionBridge for WordPress](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/wordpress-discussion-bridge) | Publish between WordPress and Discourse and show discussions on your site. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.wp.3`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/wordpress-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.wp.3) |
| [DiscussionBridge for Astro](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/astro-discussion-bridge) | Connect a plain Astro or Starlight site to your forum. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.astro.3`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/astro-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.astro.3) |
| [DiscussionBridge for Hugo](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/hugo-discussion-bridge) | Publish with Hugo and show Discourse discussions on your pages. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.hugo.4`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/hugo-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.hugo.4) |
| [DiscussionBridge for Statamic](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/statamic-discussion-bridge) | Connect a Statamic site using Flat, Eloquent DB, or static generation. | [`v0.4.0-alpha.1.statamic.4`](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/statamic-discussion-bridge/tree/v0.4.0-alpha.1.statamic.4) |

Want to see how it works? [Explore the demos](https://demo.discussionbridge.dev/).
For setup, see the [documentation](https://docs.discussionbridge.dev/). To ask a question, visit the
[community forum](https://forum.discussionbridge.dev/).

DiscussionBridge is in Alpha. See [Versions and Live Status](https://docs.discussionbridge.dev/versions-and-live-status/) for
source tags, checks, and the available deployment records. If you're building
an integration, start with the [Adapter Protocol](https://github.com/DiscussionBridge/discussionbridge-adapter-contract).
